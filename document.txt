Anthropic is an AI safety company founded in 2021 by Dario Amodei, 
Daniela Amodei, and other former members of OpenAI. The company is 
headquartered in San Francisco, California.

Anthropic's main product is Claude, an AI assistant designed to be 
helpful, harmless, and honest. Claude can help with writing, analysis, 
coding, math, and many other tasks.

Claude is available in several versions. Claude Haiku is the fastest 
and most affordable version, suitable for simple tasks. Claude Sonnet 
balances speed and intelligence, making it ideal for most use cases. 
Claude Opus is the most intelligent version, best for complex tasks 
that require deep reasoning.

Anthropic offers Claude through a web interface at claude.ai, and also 
through an API that developers can use to build applications. The API 
allows developers to integrate Claude into their own products and 
services.

Anthropic has raised significant funding from investors including Google 
and Spark Capital. The company focuses heavily on AI safety research 
alongside its commercial products. Their research includes work on 
interpretability, which aims to understand what is happening inside AI 
models.

Claude uses a context window of up to 200,000 tokens, which allows it 
to process very long documents in a single request. This is one of the 
largest context windows available among commercial AI models.
